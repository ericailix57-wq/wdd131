Mosera Automobile Site Plan

Site Name

Mosera Automobile

I chose this name because it is the registered brand name for my luxury hypercar business. It represents power, elegance, and African innovation.

Domain Idea: mosera-auto.com

Site Purpose

The website will serve as a digital showroom for Mosera Automobile. Its primary purpose is to showcase the exclusive design and technical specifications of our new hypercar model. It will also provide a reservation system for interested clients to join the waitlist.

Scenarios

  • "I am a car enthusiast; what are the horsepower and speed specifications of the Mosera model?"
  • "I am interested in buying this car; how do I contact the sales team or make a reservation?"

Typography

Heading Font: Playfair Display

Body Font: Roboto

I chose Playfair Display for headings because it is a serif font that feels expensive and luxurious. Roboto is used for body text because it is clean and easy to read on mobile devices.
